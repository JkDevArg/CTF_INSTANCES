The Music of the Mushroom Kingdom

Few melodies are as instantly recognizable as the Super Mario Bros. overworld theme. Composed by Koji Kondo in 1985, it was written to match the bouncy feel of the gameplay — the music actually speeds up when your timer runs low.

Kondo wrote distinct themes for the overworld, underground, underwater and castle stages, all within the tight memory limits of the NES. It's a masterclass in doing a lot with very little.
