Where It All Started: Donkey Kong

Released in 1981, Donkey Kong was a smash hit in arcades and the birthplace of Mario. The player climbs girders and ladders, dodging barrels thrown by a big ape, to reach the top.

A surprise success

Nintendo had a warehouse full of unsold Radar Scope cabinets. Rather than scrap them, they converted the machines to run a new game. That game was Donkey Kong, and it saved Nintendo's American operation.

Legacy

Donkey Kong introduced the idea of a story-driven platformer with a clear goal, an obstacle-filled stage, and a villain. Mario, Donkey Kong and Pauline all went on to star in dozens of games of their own.
