Mario Trivia

  • The clouds and the bushes in Super Mario Bros. are the same sprite, just colored differently.
  • Mario's full name, jokingly stated by Nintendo, is "Mario Mario".
  • The famous theme was composed by Koji Kondo.
  • There is a "minus world" glitch in the original game — an endless water level reached through a specific wall clip.
  • Hidden 1-Up blocks are scattered through the game. Some are completely invisible until bumped from underneath.
  • The fireball bounces at a fixed angle, which speedrunners use to plan precise enemy kills.
