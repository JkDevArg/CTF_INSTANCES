The Cast of Characters

Mario

The cheerful plumber and hero of the Mushroom Kingdom. Famous for his jumps, his red cap and his endless quest to rescue Princess Peach.

Luigi

Mario's taller, younger brother. He jumps a little higher and slides a little more. Often braver than he gets credit for.

Princess Peach

The ruler of the Mushroom Kingdom. Kind and resourceful, even if Bowser keeps kidnapping her.

Bowser

The spiky-shelled king of the Koopas and Mario's arch-nemesis. Breathes fire and commands an army of Goombas and Koopa Troopas.

Toad & Yoshi

Toad is Peach's loyal attendant; Yoshi is the friendly dinosaur who lets Mario ride on his back and gobble up enemies.
