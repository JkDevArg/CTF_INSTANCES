A Guide to Power-Ups

Super Mushroom

Turns small Mario into Super Mario, doubling his size and letting him take one extra hit before shrinking.

Fire Flower

Grants Mario the power to throw bouncing fireballs at enemies. Easily the most satisfying way to clear a screen of Goombas.

Starman

Brief invincibility with a catchy tune. Run through everything and watch the points pile up — but don't fall in a pit, the star won't save you there!

1-Up Mushroom

The green mushroom. Grab it for an extra life. Often hidden in invisible blocks that only appear when you jump in just the right spot...
