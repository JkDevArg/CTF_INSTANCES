In Defense of Luigi

Always "Player 2", forever in green. But Luigi has quietly become a fan favorite. His floaty jump makes him a joy in platformers, and his own series of ghost-hunting adventures gave him a personality all his own: nervous, loyal, and unexpectedly brave.

So next time someone picks Mario, grab the green cap. You might be surprised.
