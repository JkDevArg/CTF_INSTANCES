How Mario Was Born

Mario first appeared in 1981 as the hero of the arcade game Donkey Kong. Back then he wasn't even called Mario — he was known simply as "Jumpman", a carpenter trying to rescue a girl from a giant ape.

From carpenter to plumber

The character was designed by Shigeru Miyamoto. Limited by the blocky graphics of the era, Miyamoto gave him a cap (so he didn't have to draw hair), a moustache (so he didn't have to draw a mouth), and overalls (so his arm movements were easy to see while jumping). These constraints accidentally created one of the most recognizable characters in history.

The name "Mario"

According to popular legend, the character was renamed after Mario Segale, the landlord of Nintendo of America's warehouse. By the 1983 game Mario Bros., Jumpman had become Mario the plumber, joined by his brother Luigi.

In 1985, Super Mario Bros. launched on the Nintendo Entertainment System and changed video games forever.
