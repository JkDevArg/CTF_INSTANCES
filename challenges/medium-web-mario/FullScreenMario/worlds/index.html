Eight Worlds, One Princess

The original Super Mario Bros. is built from eight worlds of four levels each. Every world ends in a castle guarded by a fake Bowser — until world 8, where the real showdown finally takes place.

Along the way you'll cross overworlds, dark underground caverns, water levels, and bridges over lava. Warp pipes let the brave (or impatient) skip ahead, if they know where to look.
